Definitions and limits

An incident is one row of the City's subway delay log after exact duplicates are removed. Minutes of delay is the figure published for the incident; many incidents log zero minutes.

Last 12 months means the 12 months ending with the latest data; prior means the 12 months before that. Major incidents are those of 30 minutes or more.

Minutes are train delay as logged, not customer-minutes: the data has no ridership or train counts, so a short delay at a busy time and a longer one overnight count by their logged minutes only.

Station names are cleaned, so both platforms of a station count as one. Rows that name a stretch of track or a whole line stay in line and total figures but are not ranked as stations. Excel years hold one sheet per month; the pipeline reads every sheet.

This page is rebuilt automatically from the City's open data by the pipeline's scheduled workflow; the refreshed date above is when that last ran. The City updates the data on its own schedule, so the data-through date can be earlier.
